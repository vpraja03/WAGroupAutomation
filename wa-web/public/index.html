WhatsApp groups

Scan the QR code with your own WhatsApp. Then upload an Excel sheet with Name, Mobile, and Group. A new group is created, or new numbers are added when that group already exists.

Preparing a QR code…

  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. Tap Linked devices, then Link a device.
  3. Scan this code. Groups are created from that account.
